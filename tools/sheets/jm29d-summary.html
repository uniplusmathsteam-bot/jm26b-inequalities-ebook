Mid-point & Intercept Theorems

Colour code: equal parts you are given parallel lines what the theorem gives you

1Mid-point theorem[mid-pt. theorem]

If M and N are the mid-points of AB and AC,

AM = MB and AN = NC

then

MN // BC  and  MN = ½ BC

Two copies of MN fill BC: so BC = 2MN.

Spot itTwo mid-points on two sides of a triangle. Use it to halve or double a length, or to prove lines parallel.

2Intercept theorem[intercept theorem]

In a triangle

If AE = EB and EF // BC, then AF = FC

A line through one mid-point, parallel to a side, meets the third side at its mid-point.

Across three parallel lines

If L₁ // L₂ // L₃ and AB = BC, then DE = EF

Equal parts cut on one line give equal parts on any other line across them.

Spot itOne mid-point (or equal parts) and parallel lines. It gives another mid-point (two equal lengths).
