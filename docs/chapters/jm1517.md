The jump list uses the printed numbers for Section A (1–46) and B1–B46 for Section B, because both parts restart at 1. The top bar follows the four blocks printed in the notes: 1 Congruence, 2 Similarity, 3 Congruence, 4 Similarity. Page 1 is the summary of the tests and is not a question.

The question pages are a copy of the intensive PDF with the red answer boxes blanked, so the printed key is not sitting under the reveal. Each answer region is that blank hole, widened into empty paper for the step buttons and stopped before the figure. Several boxes on one question are one sequence of steps.

## Class slides

`books/jm1517/class/slides.html` is the intensive-class PowerPoint, one page at a time. It is live at <https://uniplusmathsteam-bot.github.io/jm26b-inequalities-ebook/books/jm1517/class/slides.html> (add `#6` for slide 6), although the book itself is still a draft and not in the library. Locally, open it from a server in `books/jm1517` (`python -m http.server`) at `class/slides.html`. Arrow keys move between the 27 slides. Changes are made one slide at a time from here. Its "Play animated" button opens the Manim deck at the same slide.

The page reads the published copies in `class/slides/` and `class/deck/`, not `reference/`, which is gitignored. After a rebuild, refresh them with `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/publish_class.py` (27 slide PNGs, then the deck's `index.html` and `index_assets/`, about 50 MB) and bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`.

The Manim deck is built in `reference/class/deck/index.html`. It replays the PowerPoint's own animations: 843 clicks over 27 slides, one manim-slides step per click (870 steps in all, counting each slide's opening view). Right arrow or space plays the next click. Press S for speaker view, whose notes give the slide, the click count, and the minute mark that keeps a 120-minute lecture on time. The deck is gitignored output, so rebuild it from the PowerPoint:

1. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/dump_timeline.py <deck.pptx> books/jm1517/reference/class/pptx/timeline.json` reads every shape and effect.
2. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/export_layers.py <deck.pptx> books/jm1517/reference/class/pptx/timeline.json books/jm1517/reference/class/layers` cuts each slide into an opaque background, one transparent PNG per animated shape, and merged static layers in z-order, at 1920 x 1080. `tools/pptx_class/check_layers.py books/jm1517/reference/class/layers` stacks them and compares the result with PowerPoint's own render; every slide matches to within 2 levels.
3. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/build_deck.py --workers 6` renders `tools/manim/jm1517_pptx.py` (scenes `S01` to `S27`) and converts them into the offline HTML deck. This takes about 10 minutes.

The animation rules were read off a PowerPoint export of slide 6 (`tools/pptx_class/probe_video.py`). Every Wipe is 0.5 s at a constant speed, with a soft edge of about 12 px. Direction 3 reveals from the bottom up, and direction 4 (the three "corr." chips on slides 3 and 16) from left to right. Motion paths are straight 2 s moves that accelerate through the first half and brake through the second. The maths is PowerPoint's own equation rendering, kept as images, not LaTeX.

`tools/manim/jm1517_class.py` is an earlier, hand-drawn Manim pass of the same deck. `python -X utf8 tools/render_class.py` writes `books/jm1517/class/<Scene>.mp4` and joins them into `books/jm1517/class/intensive-class.mp4`.

## Guides

Only four questions are filmed. Each one is a motion already used in the quadrilateral guides and in the lesson slides, on a figure of a few straight lines, and `tools/audit_guides.py jm1517` reports all four clean.

| Question | Why it was filmed | Left out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Equal angles at each end of the common side PR. The two triangles fold across PR (ASA), as in the congruence slides. | |
| A2 | Equal hypotenuses and the altitude to the base. The left triangle folds onto the right (RHS). | |
| A12 | No printed figure. Corresponding vertices are coloured, the 41° and 27° slide across, and the angle sum gives 112°. This is the corresponding-angle lesson from the similarity slides. | |
| B24 | The 5-12-13 triangle with the two given right angles. The right angles slide into correspondence, then 31.2 cm and 28.8 cm replace the unknowns. | |

Not filmed, because the figure is too easy to mis-draw or the motion is not one of the clean folds and slides: the hearts (similarity slide, question 10), regular pentagons and the hexagon, the hexagram, the paper boats, the prism with water, and any question whose given marks sit on a scanned figure that a fold would not land on exactly.

## Known before release

The workings are the red-box keys. A1, A2, A6, A10, A12 and B24 are set by hand from those boxes. The rest are read off the same boxes; a few pentagon lines are still rough and should be checked before the book is set to live. Do not publish until that check is done.

`node tools/qa/check_book.mjs jm1517` reports no failures. Many answers scroll inside a small clear patch, because the figure fills the question, and a few narrow patches shrink a line to about 50%. That is accepted for this draft. Opening the page under those patches would cut through the figure.
