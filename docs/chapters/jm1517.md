The jump list uses the printed numbers for Section A (1–46) and B1–B46 for Section B, because both parts restart at 1. The top bar follows the four blocks printed in the notes: 1 Congruence, 2 Similarity, 3 Congruence, 4 Similarity. Page 1 is the summary of the tests and is not a question.

The question pages are a copy of the intensive PDF with the red answer boxes blanked, so the printed key is not sitting under the reveal. Each answer region is that blank hole, widened into empty paper for the step buttons and stopped before the figure. Several boxes on one question are one sequence of steps.

## Class slides

`books/jm1517/class/slides.html` plays the intensive class as the Manim deck, one PowerPoint click at a time. It is live at <https://uniplusmathsteam-bot.github.io/jm26b-inequalities-ebook/books/jm1517/class/slides.html> and has a card in the library's Slides section (listed in `books/slides.json`, separate from `books/catalog.json`), although the book itself is still a draft and not in the library. Its Library button goes back to the shelf. `#6` opens slide 6, and `#6.3` opens it after its third click; the address follows along as the class moves. Locally, open it from a server in `books/jm1517` (`python -m http.server`) at `class/slides.html`. Right arrow, space, Page Down (a clicker) or › plays the next click, left arrow or ‹ goes back, F is full screen, and the strip below jumps to a slide. The counter reads "Slide 6 / 27 · step 3 / 50"; the step map comes from the deck's speaker notes, so it never needs editing. Changes are made one slide at a time from here.

The page reads the published copies in `class/deck/` and `class/thumbs/`, not `reference/`, which is gitignored. After a rebuild, refresh them with `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/publish_class.py` (the deck's `index.html` and `index_assets/`, about 58 MB, and one thumbnail per slide cut from the last frame of its last step) and bump `CACHE` in `sw.js`. No PowerPoint screenshot is published.

The Manim deck is built in `reference/class/native/deck/index.html`. Every slide is drawn by Manim on a black background (Manim's usual look), nothing is a screenshot: text and equations are XeLaTeX in the deck's own fonts (Lucida Calligraphy, Lucida Handwriting, Cambria Math, Aptos), shapes, freeforms and highlighter ink are vectors, and the exam-paper pictures are retyped and redrawn. Greys flip for the dark page (black ink becomes white), strong colours are lifted, and pale fills become a see-through wash of the same hue. Highlighter ink is a deep wash drawn just below the lowest text or picture it covers, so the marked words stay white; below the whole slide it would vanish from the animated frames. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/deck_sheets.py` puts the last frame of every published slide on three contact sheets in `reference/class/native/qa/` for a quick look over the deck. `JM1517_LIGHT=1` keeps PowerPoint's white page for the comparison checks. It keeps all 843 PowerPoint clicks over 27 slides, one manim-slides step per click (870 steps in all, counting each slide's opening view). When a slide opens, what PowerPoint shows at the start is built in quickly; then right arrow or space plays the next click. Text is written in, ink and lines are drawn along their pen direction, filled shapes draw their border and fill, and motion paths are straight 2 s moves that accelerate through the first half and brake through the second (read off a PowerPoint export of slide 6 with `tools/pptx_class/probe_video.py`). Press S for speaker view, whose notes give the slide, the click count, and the minute mark that keeps a 120-minute lecture on time. The deck is gitignored output, so rebuild it from the PowerPoint:

1. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/dump_timeline.py <deck.pptx> books/jm1517/reference/class/pptx/timeline.json` reads every effect.
2. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/pptx_scene.py <deck.pptx> books/jm1517/reference/class/pptx/scene.json` reads every shape out of the slide XML: text runs and equations with their inherited styles and bullets, preset and freeform geometry, InkML strokes, pictures, transforms and theme colours.
3. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/measure_leaves.py <deck.pptx> books/jm1517/reference/class/pptx/scene.json books/jm1517/reference/class/pptx/boxes.json` exports each text box and ink stroke alone through PowerPoint and records its ink box, which places the XeLaTeX text exactly. `tools/pptx_class/extract_pictures.py` saves the cropped pictures to `reference/class/pictures/` (used only to measure from).
4. `python -X utf8 tools/pptx_class/build_deck.py --native --workers 6` renders `tools/manim/jm1517_native.py` (scenes `N01` to `N27`) and converts them into the offline HTML deck.

`tools/manim/jm1517_native.py` builds the shapes and replays the timeline. The pictures are in `tools/manim/jm1517_figs/`, one module per unique picture (named after its first slide, such as `s6_8`), written with the drawing kit in `tools/manim/jm1517_draw.py`: vertices snap to the original's lines, labels and text rows snap to its ink. `tools/pptx_class/fig_check.py s6_8` renders one picture over its original (red is only in the original, blue only in the redraw), `tools/pptx_class/fig_grid.py s6_8` shows the original with a pixel grid for reading coordinates, and `tools/pptx_class/render_static.py 6` compares a whole slide's final state with PowerPoint's render.

The older image replay (`tools/manim/jm1517_pptx.py`, built with `build_deck.py` without `--native` from the layers that `tools/pptx_class/export_layers.py` cuts out of PowerPoint) is kept for comparison; `publish_class.py --replay` publishes it instead.

`tools/manim/jm1517_class.py` is an earlier, hand-drawn Manim pass of the same deck. `python -X utf8 tools/render_class.py` writes `books/jm1517/class/<Scene>.mp4` and joins them into `books/jm1517/class/intensive-class.mp4`.

## Guides

Only four questions are filmed. Each one is a motion already used in the quadrilateral guides and in the lesson slides, on a figure of a few straight lines, and `tools/audit_guides.py jm1517` reports all four clean.

| Question | Why it was filmed | Left out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Equal angles at each end of the common side PR. The two triangles fold across PR (ASA), as in the congruence slides. | |
| A2 | Equal hypotenuses and the altitude to the base. The left triangle folds onto the right (RHS). | |
| A12 | No printed figure. Corresponding vertices are coloured, the 41° and 27° slide across, and the angle sum gives 112°. This is the corresponding-angle lesson from the similarity slides. | |
| B24 | The 5-12-13 triangle with the two given right angles. The right angles slide into correspondence, then 31.2 cm and 28.8 cm replace the unknowns. | |

Not filmed, because the figure is too easy to mis-draw or the motion is not one of the clean folds and slides: the hearts (similarity slide, question 10), regular pentagons and the hexagon, the hexagram, the paper boats, the prism with water, and any question whose given marks sit on a scanned figure that a fold would not land on exactly.

## Known before release

The workings are the red-box keys. A1, A2, A6, A10, A12 and B24 are set by hand from those boxes. The rest are read off the same boxes; a few pentagon lines are still rough and should be checked before the book is set to live. Do not publish until that check is done.

`node tools/qa/check_book.mjs jm1517` reports no failures. Many answers scroll inside a small clear patch, because the figure fills the question, and a few narrow patches shrink a line to about 50%. That is accepted for this draft. Opening the page under those patches would cut through the figure.
