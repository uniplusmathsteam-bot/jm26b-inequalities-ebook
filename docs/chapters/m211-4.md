Vector (cross) product, q1–q14, one section. Page 1 is the concept notes; the questions start on page 2. Only the answer PDF was supplied. The question paper was made with `tools/make_que.py --red-text list --stamp ANSWER` (the stamp is in capitals here). A stray black "S" in the left margin of page 2 (also in the ANS) was removed from the made QUE.

Notation: vectors are bold (`\mathbf{a}`, `\mathbf{i}`), a vector between two points has an arrow (`\overrightarrow{AB}`), and a modulus uses stretching bars (`\left|...\right|`) so the bars cover arrows, fractions and determinants. Determinants are `vmatrix`. `solutions.js` has small helpers for this (`V`, `AR`, `abs`, `det`, `minor`).

Two slips in the teacher's working were made consistent: q9(a) writes $|4\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}|$ right after computing $4\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}$ (same modulus, 6); q10(a) prints the $\mathbf{j}$ term of $\overrightarrow{BA}$ as $\mathbf{i}$.
