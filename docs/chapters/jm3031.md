## Source facts

- Pages 1–2 are formula summaries, not questions.
- Multiple choice is one run of 1–46, and question 36 is not on the paper. Probability questions are 1–26. Measures of central tendency are 27–46.
- Short questions are 1–17 (probability) then 18–30 (central tendency), on the same numbering.
- The question paper has no red frames. The answer workings are the red boxes on the answer paper, and each box is a picture, so the text layer does not contain the working.
- The correct multiple-choice letter is the starred option in the answer text layer (`*A.` and so on).

## Special cases

- Short-question anchors are `^N.` in `extract.anchors`. A label such as S3 would otherwise match the running header.
- Answer regions are the blank beside the options, or the blank under the question. s23 sits under the histogram, at `[60, 680, 550, 808]`, so the reveal does not cover the graph.
- Trees and outcome tables follow the probability books. Several red boxes on one question open as one sequence.
- `minScale` is left at the reader default. Lines are short enough to wrap.

## Graphics

Three concept films from JM30 (B) sit in front of the printed pages (`frontPages`): the probability formula, the tree diagram, and tabulation. Each clip plays, then the next one starts.

Solution guides use the JM30 (B) tree and table guide, and only where that guide already draws the same kind of picture. A cell is coloured only when every cell with that label is favourable, so a repeated sum cannot highlight the wrong cell.

| Question | Scene | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11, 12, 16–19 | `Q11`, `Q12`, `Q16`–`Q19` | The outcome table fills, the diagonal is crossed where a card cannot be drawn twice, and the favourable cells turn pink. |
| 13–15 | `Q13`–`Q15` | The tree grows one stage at a time. Exactly two boys, a mark of −1, and a total of at least $70 are the pink outcomes. |
| S6, S7 | `S6`, `S7` | Three-stage trees. S7 is the same true/false then A/B/C tree as JM30 (B) question 16. |
| S8–S10 | `S8`–`S10` | Wallet totals, a product table, and Sara’s wrapping table. S10 is the same film as JM30 (B) question 17, including the two colours struck out in part (b). |

Questions 1–6 are counting films on a blank canvas (`jm3031_count`). The counts are the answer key: 8/7 past 1, no M in ELEPHANT, eight odd digits, four vowels in UNIVERSITY, 14 black or yellow balls, and 5 marbles that are not yellow. Questions 40–43 open an interactive graphic: subtracting, dividing, or rewriting every value moves the mean, the median and the mode together, and the graphic locks on the answer-key result.

Expected-value questions, and the central-tendency questions that need a printed chart, are still the written working.
