## Source facts

- Pages 1–2 are formula summaries, not questions.
- Multiple choice is one run of 1–46. Probability questions are 1–26. Measures of central tendency are 27–46. Question 36 is printed starred, as `*36.`, so the lone-number anchor misses it.
- Short questions are 1–17 (probability) then 18–30 (central tendency), on the same numbering.
- The question paper has no red frames. The answer workings are the red boxes on the answer paper, and each box is a picture, so the text layer does not contain the working.
- The correct multiple-choice letter is the starred option in the answer text layer (`*A.` and so on).

## Special cases

- Short-question anchors are `^N.` in `extract.anchors`. A label such as S3 would otherwise match the running header. q36 is anchored with `^\*36\.`.
- On page 14, q35's options leave no blank, and the blank below them belongs to q36. q35's region is the strip under its own options, `[164, 424, 550, 558]`, with `space: 160`. The reader places the step controls against the printed part of a region only, because past it the page image shows the next question.
- Answer regions are the blank beside the options, or the blank under the question. s23 sits under the histogram, at `[60, 680, 550, 808]`, so the reveal does not cover the graph.
- Trees and outcome tables follow the probability books. Several red boxes on one question open as one sequence.
- `minScale` is left at the reader default. Lines are short enough to wrap.

## Graphics

Three concept films from JM30 (B) sit in front of the printed pages (`frontPages`): the probability formula, the tree diagram, and tabulation. Each clip plays, then the next one starts.

Solution guides are classroom slide films (`tools/manim/jm3031_slides.py`), in the dark theme of the concept films. Each film is 3–6 slides with large type: the question, the picture, the favourable outcomes coloured, then the fraction and an "Answer X" badge. They show the key moves, not every written step. Each slide explains itself without a voice-over: a numbered caption in the bar along the foot says what the slide shows, and every highlight (coloured cell, gold path, cross, right/wrong ring, dimmed outcome) is named on screen beside a swatch of it. Content stays above y 640 so the bar never covers it. `render_media.py` writes each film's slide ends into `book.json` as `stops`, and the guide player pauses at every stop: ▶, ›, space, → or Page Down plays the next slide; ‹, ← or Page Up goes back one; F is full screen. A presentation clicker works. "Pause each slide" switches stepping off, and the choice is remembered.
